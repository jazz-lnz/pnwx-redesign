Payment Methods

Credit Cards Open Accounts (US Only) Wire Transfer Check / Money Order

Credit Cards: A 0.4% merchant fee applies. International orders usually require wire transfers ($50 fee).

Open Accounts: Available for US customers only. First opening order must be $500+ USD.

Checks: Subject to a 10-day clearing delay and a $35 returned check fee.

Some suppliers now require full pre-payment at the time of order.

Cancellations & Returns

Cancellations: Must be made same-day. "Rush" or "Same-Day" shipments cannot be cancelled.

Restocking: Authorized returns incur a 20% to 50% fee plus freight charges both ways.

Non-Returnable: Custom items (lead aprons, markers, etc.) and authorized returns after 30 days are not accepted.

Refusal: Refusing delivery results in return shipping and restocking fees being deducted from any credit.

Warranties & Liability

Warranties: Managed by the manufacturer. We provide assistance for US-based claims only; international customers must contact manufacturers directly.

Delivery: Buyer must note container damage before signing. Large items require a dock/forklift unless inside delivery is arranged in advance.

Compliance: Setup, licensing, and legal usage of X-ray equipment are the sole responsibility of the buyer.
